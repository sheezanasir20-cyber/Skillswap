# Content and Voice Guide

## Voice
Friendly, capable, plain-spoken. Treat members as peers. Be encouraging without hype.

## Principles
- Say what happened.
- Say what happens next.
- Use familiar words.
- Avoid blame.
- Do not imply verification, guaranteed quality, or guaranteed availability.
- Use “member” or “person,” not “resource” or “lead.”

## Preferred language
| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Find a learning partner | Unlock your potential |
| Skills you can teach | Your superpowers |
| Skills you want to learn | Your dream skills |
| Send request | Initiate connection journey |
| Session | Transformational experience |
| No matches yet | Nothing here! |
| Try a broader search | Optimize your discovery parameters |

## UI examples
- Empty discover: “No matches found with these filters. Try another skill or widen your availability.”
- Request sent: “Connection request sent.”
- No sessions: “No sessions yet. Once you connect with someone, you can plan a time together.”
- Error: “We couldn’t save your changes. Check your connection and try again.”
- Report confirmation: “Report received. A moderator will review it.”
- Permission: “You don’t have access to this page.”

## Dates and time
Use readable local dates and always identify timezone when ambiguity is possible. Prefer “20 Nov, 6:30 PM (PKT)” to an unlabeled timestamp.
